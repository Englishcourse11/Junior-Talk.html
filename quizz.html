📘 Materi Causative

Causative verbs adalah kata kerja yang digunakan ketika seseorang menyebabkan orang lain melakukan sesuatu, atau membuat sesuatu terjadi. Ada beberapa causative yang umum: make, let, have, get, help.

1. MAKE

Pola: make + orang + verb-1 (tanpa to)

Artinya: memaksa / membuat seseorang melakukan sesuatu.

"The teacher makes the students study hard."

2. LET

Pola: let + orang + verb-1

Artinya: mengizinkan / membiarkan.

"My mother lets me go to the party."

3. HAVE

Pola: have + orang + verb-1 (aktif) / have + objek + verb-3 (pasif)

Artinya: menyuruh / meminta seseorang melakukan sesuatu.

"I had the technician fix my laptop."

4. GET

Pola: get + orang + to + verb-1

Artinya: membujuk / membuat seseorang melakukan sesuatu.

"She got her brother to clean the room."

5. HELP

Pola: help + orang + (to) + verb-1

Artinya: membantu seseorang melakukan sesuatu. "To" boleh dipakai atau dihilangkan.

"He helped me (to) carry the boxes."

💡 Catatan: causative "have" dan "get" juga bisa digunakan untuk passive causative (have/get + objek + verb-3). Contoh: I had my hair cut.

🎤 Speaking Assignment: My Favorite Dish

Dear Students,

Today, I would like you to talk about your favorite dish and explain how to make it.

  1. Choose Your Favorite Dish — Think about your favorite food or dish. It can be a traditional dish, a local specialty, or any food you really enjoy eating.
  2. Write Your Script — Write a short script in English to answer: What is your favorite dish? Why do you like it? What ingredients do you need? What are the steps to prepare and cook it?
  3. Practice Your Speaking — Read your script aloud several times. Pay attention to pronunciation, fluency, and confidence.
  4. Record Your Video — Record a video of yourself presenting your favorite dish and explaining how to make it. You may show the ingredients or demonstrate the cooking steps.
  5. Send Your Video to My WhatsApp — After you finish recording, send your video to my WhatsApp. Make sure your video is complete and your voice can be heard clearly.

Remember: This assignment is not only about writing a script. It is also an opportunity to improve your English-speaking skills, express your ideas, and build your confidence. Do your best, speak confidently, and enjoy the process of learning English! Good luck, everyone! 🌟
